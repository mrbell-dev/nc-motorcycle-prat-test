# NC Motorcycle Knowledge Test Prep

A phone-friendly practice quiz for the North Carolina motorcycle knowledge test.

- 261 multiple-choice questions, each tied to a page in the NC Motorcyclists' Handbook (13th edition, the version NCDMV links) or the NCDMV Motorcycle & Moped page
- Questions sorted by the handbook's own sections and subsections, with a filter for each
- After every answer: why the right answer is right and why each wrong choice is wrong, plus an optional hint before you answer
- Mock test (25 questions), new-questions-only mode, review of missed questions and a drill of your weakest topics
- A report at the end of every session: score by section and subsection, your weakest areas, the questions you missed with their handbook pages, and a button to drill those areas
- Diagrams for lane positions, curves, group formation, track crossings, slow turns and oncoming left turns
- Your answers and reports stay in your own browser's local storage; nothing is sent anywhere

This is an unofficial study aid. It is not affiliated with NCDMV, and the real test words things its own way. Check the official handbook and NCDMV for current rules:

- NC Motorcyclists' Handbook: https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/driver-licenses/new-drivers/Documents/motorcyclist-handbook.pdf
- NCDMV Motorcycle & Moped Privileges: https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/driver-licenses/motorcycle/Pages/default.aspx
