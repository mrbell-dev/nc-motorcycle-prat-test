Study sources

Every answer here is tied to a page in the official handbook, shown after you answer. Page numbers are the ones printed on the handbook's pages. NCDMV still links the 13th edition (September 2007) as its Motorcyclists' Handbook, and lists no newer one.

  • NC Motorcyclists' Handbook (PDF): the source for the test. Its Test Yourself questions and the sample test questions on p. 42 are the best extra practice.
  • NCDMV Motorcycle & Moped Privileges: endorsement and permit rules, helmet law.
  • NCDMV Handbooks: where NCDMV lists its current handbooks.
